**Leveraging Python for Building a Logistics Simulator @ Glovo**  
Explore how Glovo leveraged the Python ecosystem to develop a digital twin of its logistics operations spanning 600+ cities in 22 countries. In this talk, we examine the integration of discrete‑event simulation, optimization algorithms, and meta‑heuristics to dynamically adjust and optimize operational parameters. Attendees will learn how these simulation techniques can drive operational improvements and gain practical insights from the lessons learned during development.

**Manuel Bolivar - Staff Data Scientist**  
With a background in Operations Research and Statistics, Manuel brings over 10 years of experience applying scientific techniques to solve business challenges across tech companies, particularly in logistics and customer personalization. Passionate about automated decision‑making and causal inference, he currently leverages DS techniques to improve recommendation systems and drive higher user engagement @ Glovo.
